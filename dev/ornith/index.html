    Ornith-1.5-122B¶

    Ornith AI's Ornith-1.5-122B is an agentic and coding model on the Qwen3.5 mixture-of-experts layout. About 10B of its 122B parameters run for each token, and a decode token reads about 4.76 GB of our 4.0-bit EXL3 pack (arithmetic, from the headers). On one DGX Spark the engine decodes it at 37.2 to 38.4 tok/s plain and at 85.3 to 124.8 tok/s with its MTP layer and the lookup (measured, 2026-10-20). Its support ships in release 0.13.0. This page says what is different about it and what is measured; Adding a model describes the adapter path it came in through.

    The model¶

    part Ornith-1.5-122B Flash-Next
    layers 48: 36 Gated DeltaNet, 12 gated attention 48: 36 Gated DeltaNet, 12 attention
    MLP 256 experts a layer, 8 routed and 1 shared (sigmoid-gated) 512 experts, 10 routed and 1 shared
    residual stream one 4 streams of 2,560
    attention dense, 2 KV heads of 256 sparse past 2,051 tokens
    KV a token 24,576 bytes, 2,048 more for the MTP layer 29,952 bytes
    context 262,144 tokens 262,144
    draft layer one MTP layer one MTP layer
    images a Qwen3.5 vision tower, 27 blocks a vision tower
    weights served our EXL3 pack at 4.0 bits a weight turboderp's EXL3 packs

    Ornith-1.5-122B beside Flash-Next.

    engine/models/qwen3_5_moe holds the trunk. It reads the configuration strictly and the EXL3 pack with its per-expert projections and mixed rates. The row step and the prefill run on Flash-Next's EXL3 kernels with one residual stream, and the router kernel takes up to 512 experts. The reference forward (ref.py) matches transformers on tiny random configs of both Qwen3.5 MoE shapes. The tokenizer has 248,320 ids, so the lookup keeps a suffix store of its own.

    The pack¶

    Our pack has every projection at 4 bits: the routed and shared experts, the Gated DeltaNet and the attention layers. The head is at 6 bits, the MTP layer at 4, and the vision tower at 6. exllamav3 1.6.2 converted it from the BF16 checkpoint at revision e09bc0dfbe. In layers 3 to 13, 13 routed experts have weights that are all zero once cast to fp16, and stock exllamav3 stops on them; the conversion used a patch that keeps their input scales, published in the pack's repository. The repository 0xBakeer/TandemLLM-Ornith-1.5-122B-EXL3-4.0 is public, 64.6 GB, pinned by commit (92f7483c).

    The engine and the converter's forward do not agree on every token. Of the 14 positions where the top token differs, 9 have a margin under 0.5 logits, 2 a margin between 0.5 and 1, 2 between 1 and 2, and one over 4. The cause is not tested.

    Drafting¶

    The profile drafts with the pack's MTP layer as a chain of 3 and the lookup, in one tree that StairCut cuts with the pack's price file (ops/prices/ornith-1.5-122b/). After a round that StairCut cut to the anchor alone, the next 1, 2, 4, then 8 rounds take the plain step before the next draft (QWEN38_Q35M_ANCHOR_BACKOFF=8); a round that drafts starts the count over. QSE_ORN_HEAD=dflash2 drafts with incoai's DFlash2 drafter for Ornith instead (1.86 GB, Apache-2.0), with its projections in NVFP4 and its head over the 32,768 most used tokens; it takes 2.86 GiB.

    Serving¶

    One slot, a window up to 262,144 tokens, the pack's own vision tower on the EXL3 kernels (0.35 GB at load). The model thinks in a <think> block and calls tools in the XML form. The checkpoint's generation config sets the defaults: temperature 0.6, top_k 20, top_p 0.95. The conductor and Laya are on and the narrator is off; the conductor runs its default fit, which is not measured on this model yet. A start loads the pack in 65 s: the weights take 59.95 GiB, 61.16 with the MTP layer, and a start needs 93 GiB of free memory. models/ornith-1.5-122b/measurements/0.13.0-summary.json has the runs.

    The e4m3 KV cache¶

    Q35M_KV_FP8=1 keeps the 12 attention layers' keys and values as e4m3 codes with one fp32 scale a (head, token): 12,480 bytes a token of window instead of 24,576, the MTP layer's cache in bf16 (SPD-150). The output is no longer the exact model's, so the knob is off by default. Turn it on with Long-context cache (FP8) under Features in the Setup Guide's Tools step, or with QWEN38_Q35M_KV_FP8=1 at the end of ~/TandemLLM/run/ops/serve.env and ~/TandemLLM/bin/tandem restart. Its speed and cost are not measured.

    What is not measured¶

    • Requests at once: the joint verify of several slots is not built for this trunk yet (SRV-653), so the profile has one slot.
    • Prompt reading speed, first-token times, images, sampled decoding and repeat runs.
    • Any task benchmark beyond tool-eval-bench. On the 0.13.0 code, one run a row, it scored 93 and 94 on the raw path at every thinking setting, 96 with the conductor's tools profile, and 80 raw against 84 with the conductor in hard mode (docs/tool-eval-bench.md).
    • The cause of the 14 differences from the converter's forward.
    • The e4m3 KV cache's speed and its cost against the exact cache.
